Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

Watchloom does not require an account. Your personal library, progress, favorites, notes and viewing activity are stored locally on your device.

1. Information stored by Watchloom

Watchloom stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Your show library and library statuses
  • Your last watched season and episode
  • Favorites and personal notes
  • Watch activity, streaks and achievements
  • Language and notification preferences

This personal library information is not uploaded to a Watchloom-owned account or cloud server.

3. TV and episode information

Watchloom requests show, episode, cast, network, platform and rating information from TVMaze. Network requests may provide TVMaze with standard technical information such as your IP address.

Learn more in TVMaze's Privacy Policy.

4. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Watchloom schedules local episode reminders on your device. Watchloom does not operate a remote push notification account service.

5. Data deletion

You can delete Watchloom's locally stored data from:

Profile → Settings → Clear All Watchloom Data

Removing the app from your device also removes its locally stored application data, subject to your device and backup settings.

6. Children's privacy

Watchloom is not designed for children under the age of 13. The app does not provide accounts, messaging or user-generated social content.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Watchloom's features, third-party services or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, open a support request on Watchloom's GitHub repository:

Gizlilik Politikası

Yürürlük tarihi: 14 Ağustos 2026

Watchloom hesap gerektirmez. Kütüphane, ilerleme, favori, kişisel not ve izleme etkinliği verilerin cihazında yerel olarak saklanır.

Yerel veriler

Dizi kütüphanesi, son izlenen bölüm, kişisel notlar, favoriler, etkinlik geçmişi, seriler, başarımlar, dil ve bildirim tercihleri cihazında saklanır. Bu bilgiler Watchloom'a ait bir hesap veya bulut sunucusuna yüklenmez.

Veri silme

Yerel Watchloom verilerini Profil → Ayarlar → Tüm Watchloom Verilerini Sil adımlarından silebilirsin.
