Frequently asked questions

Does Watchloom require an account?

No. Guest mode keeps your library on this device. You can optionally sign in with Google, Apple or email to back up your account library, notes and history. After reinstalling, use the same Watchloom account to recover a completed cloud backup. Manage or delete your account from Profile → Account & backup. Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Why does an upcoming episode not appear?

Watchloom only displays episodes with a future date available from TVMaze. Some shows do not yet have a confirmed release date.

Why did a notification not arrive?

Confirm that notifications are enabled both in Watchloom and in iOS Settings. Opening Watchloom periodically also allows the local schedule to be refreshed.

How do I delete my information?

For guest records, open Profile → Settings → Clear Watch Data. For your signed-in account, open Profile → Account & backup → Delete account. Account deletion requires fresh authentication and a working connection; it removes cloud backup versions, the Firebase identity and the account cache on this device. If interrupted, sign back in and finish deletion. The separate guest library remains. Manage any Apple subscription separately. See the privacy policy for the minimal temporary security marker and provider retention.

Why is a banner advertisement displayed?

The free version may load bottom banner ads only after you allow limited ads in Advertising & Privacy. An active monthly Watchloom Premium subscription removes them and unlocks an unlimited library.

Why can I save only 5 shows?

The free library includes 5 shows across all statuses. Remove a saved show to make room, or open Watchloom Premium from Library or Settings for unlimited saved shows and no banner ads. Other tracking features remain free.

How do I restore or cancel Premium?

Open Watchloom Premium → Restore Purchases using the same Apple Account. Manage Subscription opens Apple’s subscription settings. If Premium ends, existing shows and progress stay on this device. Restoring Premium does not restore deleted library data.

Why is the App Store price unavailable?

Check your connection and Apple Account storefront, then use Try Again on the Premium screen. No payment is started when a price is unavailable. Contact support if the issue persists.

Can deleted data be restored?

A completed cloud backup can recover an account library after reinstalling when you sign in to the same Watchloom account. Unsaved local changes and guest records cannot be recovered by signing in. Deleting the account removes its cloud backups and cannot be undone in the app.
