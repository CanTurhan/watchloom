## App Description — English

Keep your shows, episode progress and viewing memories together with Watchloom.

Save shows to your library, remember your last watched episode, follow known upcoming releases, add notes and favorites, and review your monthly activity. Episode reminders and viewing statistics are available on the free plan too.

Free includes up to 5 saved shows across all library statuses. Optional banner ads are controlled by your Advertising & Privacy choice.

Watchloom Premium is an optional monthly auto-renewable subscription for unlimited saved and tracked shows and removal of banner ads. Your App Store displays your local price before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. The subscription renews monthly unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings. Restore an active subscription using Restore Purchases in the Premium screen.

Guest mode needs no Watchloom account and keeps your library on this device. Optionally sign in with Apple, Google or email to back up your separate account library, notes and history. Sign in to the same Watchloom account to recover a completed cloud backup after reinstalling. Manage or delete it from Profile → Account & backup. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription. Restoring a purchase alone does not recover library data. Saved shows and progress remain if Premium ends. New additions are restricted while a free library contains 5 or more shows.

Show metadata: TVmaze, CC BY-SA 4.0. Watchloom does not provide streaming video.

## App Review Notes — English

Kopyalamadan önce sağlayıcı yapılandırması, gerçek cihaz ve App Privacy kontrollerini tamamla. Aşağıdaki İngilizce metin uygulama davranışını anlatır; tek başına bir gizlilik denetimi kanıtı değildir.

Guest features require no sign-in. Optional Apple, Google and verified-email Firebase accounts add a separate private library backup. Profile → Account & backup provides verification, backup status, explicit guest import, conflict resolution, sign-out and in-app deletion with fresh reauthentication. Account deletion erases cloud versions and the Firebase identity without cancelling Apple subscriptions. This is a personal TV tracking app; it does not stream television content.

Premium is accessible from the Library count button, Settings → Watchloom Premium, or by trying to add a sixth show to a free library. The monthly product ID is com.canturhan.watchloom.premium.monthly. Premium provides ongoing unlimited show tracking and removal of banner ads. Other tracking features remain free. Existing saved shows, notes and history are preserved if a subscription ends; new additions are limited for free libraries containing 5 or more shows.

The Premium screen shows the StoreKit-localized monthly price, renewal terms, Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription. The privacy policy also opens inside the app without a network request. Subscription restoration is separate from library recovery through a completed account backup.

Free users choose whether to allow limited banner advertising in Advertising & Privacy. Refusing advertising does not block free features. The advertising SDK is not initialized before that choice, or for verified Premium access. Watchloom does not request ATT authorization. It explicitly disables AppMetrica IDFA reporting and location reporting before/after Ads SDK initialization, and disables optional app/ad analytics reporting. Vendor privacy manifests are retained, including AppMetricaAdSupport's conditional tracking declaration.
